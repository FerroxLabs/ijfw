# Book Brief: The Quiet Witness — Chapter 7 (working title)

> Domain: `book` -- non-software project, no git/CI/tests required.
> Source of truth for the manuscript's intent. Updated, not regenerated.
> Scope note: this brief is a single-chapter slice (Chapter 7) of a larger thriller already in progress. Premise, world rules, characters, and themes are inherited from the parent BOOK.md; restated here so the chapter brief survives in isolation.

## Premise

Chapter 7 is the antagonist confrontation — the moment Detective Mara Voss finally meets her stalker face-to-face and discovers he has been impersonating someone she trusts. The reader has been chasing the antagonist's identity for six chapters; this is the reveal. It is the pivot from "who is doing this" to "what does she do about it now that the cost of stopping him is personal."

## World rules

Inherited from BOOK.md. Restated for chapter context:

- Investigations in this city happen against a department culture that punishes officers who go off-radio; any move Mara makes "alone" carries career risk, not just physical risk.
- The antagonist is procedurally competent. He has read the same training Mara has. He does not slip up by accident — every clue she finds was placed.
- Identity in this book is performative: people are who they convincingly play, until something forces the mask off. The confrontation is structurally the mask-off moment.

## Characters / cast

## POV and voice

- **POV:** Close third, Mara only. The reader knows what Mara knows, no more.
- **Tense:** Past
- **Tone:** Procedural-tight in the first half; perceptual and unsettled in the second half, when the mask drops.
- **Comparable voices:** Tana French's interior procedurals meet Dennis Lehane's set-piece tension.

## Themes

The 1-3 ideas the book is really about, beneath the surface plot or argument.

- Identity is the most dangerous evidence — easiest to forge, hardest to disprove.
- The cost of trusting your own pattern-matching when the pattern was designed for you.
- Institutional process protects against strangers; this antagonist is not a stranger.
